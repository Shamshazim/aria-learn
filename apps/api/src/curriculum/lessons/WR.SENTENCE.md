## What it is

Writing one sentence that has someone doing something, with a capital at the start and a full stop at the end.

## The one idea

A sentence is a whole thought. Say it out loud first, then write the words you said.

## Common stumbles

- Writes a list of words with no verb, so nothing is happening.
- Runs several thoughts together with and, and, and.
- Leaves off the capital or the full stop, so where the thought starts and ends is invisible.

## Two concrete models

- Say it, count the words on fingers, then write one word per finger.
- A two-part frame, who and what they did, filled in aloud before anything is written.

## Worked example

Say it: the dog ran fast. Four words. Write the dog ran fast, capital on the, full stop at the end.

## Language to use

- say the whole thought
- who and what they did
- capital and full stop

## Language to avoid

- subject and predicate
- independent clause
- terminal punctuation
