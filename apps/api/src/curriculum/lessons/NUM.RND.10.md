## What it is

Replacing a whole number with the ten it is closest to, so 47 becomes 50 and 42 becomes 40, and a number ending in 5 goes up.

## The one idea

Rounding finds the nearer of the two tens a number sits between.

## Common stumbles

- Always rounds down by dropping the ones, so 47 becomes 40.
- Always rounds up to the next ten, so 42 becomes 50.
- Rounds to the nearest hundred instead of the nearest ten.

## Two concrete models

- A number line from one ten to the next with the number marked, counting the steps to each end.
- A hill drawn over the tens: numbers ending in 5 or more roll forward to the next ten, the rest roll back.

## Worked example

Round 47 to the nearest ten. It sits between 40 and 50. It is seven steps from 40 and three steps from 50, so it rounds to 50.

## Language to use

- nearest ten
- closer to
- between
- round up, round down

## Language to avoid

- approximation
- truncation
- significant figures
