## What it is

Knowing the times tables up to ten times ten quickly, without skip-counting every time.

## The one idea

Times means equal groups. Seven times six is seven groups with six in each, and it is the same as six groups of seven.

## Common stumbles

- Adds the numbers, so seven times six becomes thirteen.
- Skip-counts one jump too few and lands on thirty-six.
- Skip-counts one jump too many and lands on forty-nine.

## Two concrete models

- An array: seven rows of six dots, counted a row at a time.
- A number line: seven equal jumps of six.

## Worked example

Seven times six. Five sixes are thirty. Two more sixes are twelve. Thirty and twelve is forty-two.

## Language to use

- groups of
- rows of
- times

## Language to avoid

- product of factors
- the commutative law
- repeated addition algorithm
