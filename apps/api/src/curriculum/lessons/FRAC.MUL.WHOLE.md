## What it is

Multiplying a fraction by a whole number, such as three times two fifths.

## The one idea

Three times two fifths is three groups of two fifths. The pieces stay fifths; there are just more of them.

## Common stumbles

- Multiplies the top and the bottom, and gets six fifteenths, which is the same as two fifths.
- Writes three and two fifths, as if the three were added.
- Multiplies only the bottom, making the pieces smaller.

## Two concrete models

- Three copies of a two-fifths bar laid side by side, then the fifths counted.
- A number line in fifths with three jumps of two fifths.

## Worked example

Three times two fifths. Two fifths, three times, is six fifths. Five fifths make one whole, so six fifths is one and one fifth.

## Language to use

- groups of
- still fifths
- how many fifths altogether

## Language to avoid

- multiply the numerator by the integer
- improper fraction conversion
- scalar multiplication
