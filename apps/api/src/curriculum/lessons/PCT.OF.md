## What it is

Finding a percent of a number, such as 25 percent of 80.

## The one idea

Percent means out of a hundred. Twenty-five percent is twenty-five hundredths, which is one quarter, so 25 percent of 80 is a quarter of 80.

## Common stumbles

- Multiplies 25 by 80 and forgets to divide by a hundred, getting 2000.
- Reads "of" as "divided by" and works out 80 divided by 25.
- Takes 25 away from 80 and gets 55.

## Two concrete models

- A hundred-square with twenty-five squares shaded, showing a quarter.
- A bar of 80 split into four equal parts with one part shaded.

## Worked example

What is 25 percent of 80? Twenty-five percent is one quarter. A quarter of 80 is 80 shared into four. Twenty.

## Language to use

- out of a hundred
- ten percent is one tenth
- a quarter of

## Language to avoid

- percentage proportion equation
- multiply by the decimal equivalent
- per centum
