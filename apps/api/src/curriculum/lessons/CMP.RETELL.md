## What it is

Saying back what happened in a short story, in order, in the child's own words.

## The one idea

If you can tell it back, you understood it. The order is part of the meaning.

## Common stumbles

- Names the characters but not what they did.
- Tells the ending first and cannot recover the order.
- Repeats the story word for word without showing any understanding of it.

## Two concrete models

- First, then, last: three prompts that hold the shape of the story while the child fills it in.
- Three quick drawings, one per part, retold from the pictures.

## Worked example

The cat sat. Then the dog ran in. Last the cat ran out. Retell it: first the cat sat, then the dog came, last the cat left.

## Language to use

- what happened first
- and then
- in your own words

## Language to avoid

- narrative sequence
- summarise the plot
- story grammar
