## What it is

Writing several sentences that all stay on one point, with a first sentence that says what the point is.

## The one idea

A paragraph is one idea with its evidence. Every sentence in it has to earn its place.

## Common stumbles

- Drifts to a second topic halfway through, so the paragraph is two halves.
- Writes one long sentence joined by commas instead of several sentences.
- Gives the detail without ever stating the point it supports.

## Two concrete models

- Point, then two reasons, then a closing sentence, planned in four short phrases before drafting.
- Read each sentence back and ask whether it is about the first sentence; delete the ones that are not.

## Worked example

Point: dogs make good pets. Reason one: they are loyal. Reason two: they get you outside. Closing: that is why a dog is a good first pet.

## Language to use

- stay on one point
- does this sentence belong
- your closing sentence

## Language to avoid

- topic sentence and supporting detail
- cohesion
- discourse markers
