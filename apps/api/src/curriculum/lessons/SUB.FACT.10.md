## What it is

Knowing the take-away facts within ten, such as seven take away three, quickly and without counting every one.

## The one idea

Taking away is the addition fact backwards. Three and four make seven, so seven take away three leaves four.

## Common stumbles

- Adds the two numbers and answers ten.
- Counts back from seven but says seven as the first step, landing on five.
- Answers with the three that was taken, not the four that is left.

## Two concrete models

- A ten-frame: fill seven, cross out three, count the ones still there.
- A number line: start on seven and make three jumps back.

## Worked example

Seven take away three. Put seven counters out. Lift three away. Four are left, so seven take away three is four.

## Language to use

- take away
- how many are left
- the fact the other way round

## Language to avoid

- minuend and subtrahend
- the inverse operation
- negative counting
