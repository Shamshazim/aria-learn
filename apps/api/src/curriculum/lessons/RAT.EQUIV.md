## What it is

Completing a ratio that is equivalent to another, such as 2 : 3 = 8 : ?.

## The one idea

A ratio grows by multiplying both parts by the same number. 2 became 8 by times four, so 3 becomes 3 times four.

## Common stumbles

- Adds the same to both parts: 2 went up by 6, so 3 goes up by 6 to 9.
- Changes the first part and leaves the second as 3.
- Finds the times four and answers 4.

## Two concrete models

- A double number line: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 on top and 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 underneath.
- A recipe: two cups of flour to three of milk, made four times over.

## Worked example

2 : 3 = 8 : ?. Two times four is eight, so the ratio has been made four times bigger. Three times four is twelve. 2 : 3 = 8 : 12.

## Language to use

- for every
- times the same number
- grows together

## Language to avoid

- proportional reasoning
- multiplicative comparison
- scale factor of the ratio
