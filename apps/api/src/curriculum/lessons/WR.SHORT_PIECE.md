## What it is

Writing a short piece with a clear beginning, a middle where something happens, and an end that closes it.

## The one idea

A piece has a shape. The end has to answer something the beginning set up.

## Common stumbles

- Writes a beginning and a middle and then stops, with no ending at all.
- Spends most of the piece on setting and leaves no room for what happens.
- Ends with and then I woke up, which closes the piece without resolving anything.

## Two concrete models

- Three boxes planned before drafting: what starts it, what happens, how it lands.
- Write the ending sentence first, then write towards it.

## Worked example

Beginning: the box was on the step and nobody knew whose it was. Middle: opening it turned out to be a mistake. End: by evening the box was back on the step, and this time it was locked.

## Language to use

- how does it start
- what happens
- how does it land

## Language to avoid

- narrative arc
- denouement
- structural coherence
