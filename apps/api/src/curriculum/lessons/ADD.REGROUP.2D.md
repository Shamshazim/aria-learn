## What it is

Adding two two-digit numbers when the ones add to ten or more, so a ten has to be carried into the tens place.

## The one idea

Ten ones are one ten. When the ones column overflows, you trade the ten and the total does not change.

## Common stumbles

- Adds each column on its own and writes the two-digit ones total straight down, so forty-eight add thirty-seven becomes seven hundred and fifteen.
- Carries the wrong digit, keeping the one and carrying the five.
- Forgets the carried ten when adding the tens column.

## Two concrete models

- Place-value blocks: build both numbers, gather the ones, and physically trade ten of them for one ten rod.
- A number line: jump the whole tens first, then the ones, and see where the ten is crossed.

## Worked example

Forty-eight add thirty-seven. Eight and seven is fifteen. Fifteen is one ten and five ones, so write five and carry the ten. Four tens, three tens and the carried ten is eight tens. Eighty-five.

## Language to use

- trade ten ones for a ten
- the ones column
- carry the ten

## Language to avoid

- regroup the minuend
- place-value decomposition
- carry the surplus
