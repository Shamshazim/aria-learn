## What it is

Writing one word that captures an idea, spelled as well as the child can hear it.

## The one idea

Writing starts with getting one true word down. Spelling comes after the idea is on the page.

## Common stumbles

- Freezes because the correct spelling is unknown, so nothing is written at all.
- Writes a letter string with no vowel in it.
- Copies a word from the room instead of choosing one that fits the idea.

## Two concrete models

- Say the word slowly and write the sounds you hear, in order, and accept that.
- Choose from three spoken words, then write the chosen one, so the choosing is separate from the writing.

## Worked example

The idea is a big dog. Say dog slowly: d, o, g. Write the three sounds. That is the word.

## Language to use

- say it slowly
- write the sounds you hear
- good enough to read back

## Language to avoid

- orthography
- invented spelling
- encode the phonemes
