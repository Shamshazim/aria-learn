## What it is

Hearing three separate sounds and pushing them together into one word.

## The one idea

A word is its sounds said without gaps. Blending is closing the gaps.

## Common stumbles

- Repeats the three sounds back as three sounds instead of one word.
- Loses the middle sound, turning c-a-t into cat's neighbour ct or cot.
- Says a word that starts right but is guessed from the first sound alone.

## Two concrete models

- Say the sounds with shrinking gaps: c a t, then c-a-t, then cat.
- Move a counter for each sound and then slide the counters together as the word is said.

## Worked example

Say c, a, t with clear gaps. Now say it faster: c-a-t. Now with no gap at all: cat.

## Language to use

- push the sounds together
- say it faster
- what word is that

## Language to avoid

- phoneme
- synthesise
- articulatory
