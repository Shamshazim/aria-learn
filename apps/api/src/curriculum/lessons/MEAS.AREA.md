## What it is

Finding the area and the perimeter of a rectangle from its length and width.

## The one idea

Area is how many unit squares fit inside: length times width. Perimeter is the distance all the way round the edge: add all four sides.

## Common stumbles

- Gives the perimeter when asked for area, or the other way round.
- Adds just the length and the width once.
- Multiplies one side by itself.

## Two concrete models

- A rectangle drawn on squared paper, counting the squares inside.
- A string laid round the edge, then straightened and measured.

## Worked example

A rectangle 6 cm by 4 cm. Area: 6 rows of 4 squares, 24 square cm. Perimeter: 6 + 4 + 6 + 4, which is 20 cm.

## Language to use

- squares inside
- all the way round
- length and width

## Language to avoid

- two-dimensional measure
- linear versus square units
- area formula derivation
