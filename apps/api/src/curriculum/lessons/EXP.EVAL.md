## What it is

Working out an expression with a letter in it once the letter has a value, such as 3n + 4 when n is 5.

## The one idea

A number written against a letter means times. 3n is three lots of n, so with n as 5 it is 3 × 5.

## Common stumbles

- Writes the 3 next to the 5 and reads thirty-five.
- Adds the 3 and the 5.
- Drops the 3 and uses just 5.

## Two concrete models

- Three bags with n sweets in each, and four loose sweets.
- The expression rewritten with a times sign and the value in a box: 3 × [5] + 4.

## Worked example

3n + 4 when n is 5. 3n means 3 × n, so 3 × 5 is 15. Then 15 + 4 is 19.

## Language to use

- three lots of n
- put 5 where n is
- the number in front means times

## Language to avoid

- coefficient
- substitution into an algebraic expression
- evaluate the polynomial
