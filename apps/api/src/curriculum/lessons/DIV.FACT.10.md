## What it is

Knowing the division facts that undo the times tables, such as twenty-four divided by six.

## The one idea

Dividing asks how many groups fit, or how many go in each group. Six times four is twenty-four, so twenty-four divided by six is four.

## Common stumbles

- Multiplies instead, and answers one hundred forty-four.
- Takes six away from twenty-four and answers eighteen.
- Skip-counts towards twenty-four and stops one group short, answering three.

## Two concrete models

- Counters shared one at a time into six equal piles, then one pile counted.
- An array of twenty-four dots made into rows of six.

## Worked example

Twenty-four divided by six. Which times six makes twenty-four? Four sixes are twenty-four. So the answer is four.

## Language to use

- share equally
- how many groups of
- the times fact backwards

## Language to avoid

- dividend and divisor
- the inverse operation
- quotient
