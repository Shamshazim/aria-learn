## What it is

Knowing what each digit after the decimal point is worth: in 3.47 the 4 is four tenths and the 7 is seven hundredths.

## The one idea

Place value keeps going past the point. Each place to the right is ten times smaller, so after the ones come tenths, then hundredths.

## Common stumbles

- Reads the places backwards and says the 7 is in the tenths place.
- Says 0.4 instead of the digit 4.
- Counts the ones as the first place and says 3.

## Two concrete models

- A place-value chart with ones, the point, tenths and hundredths.
- A number line from three to four cut into ten tenths, with 3.47 between 3.4 and 3.5.

## Worked example

In 3.47, which digit is in the tenths place? The point comes after the ones. The first place after it is tenths. That digit is 4.

## Language to use

- tenths and hundredths
- the first place after the point
- ten times smaller

## Language to avoid

- decimal fraction notation
- the tenths column of the mantissa
- negative powers of ten
