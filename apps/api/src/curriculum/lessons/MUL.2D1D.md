## What it is

Multiplying a two-digit number by a one-digit number when the ones make ten or more, so tens are carried.

## The one idea

Multiply the ones, then the tens. The 4 in 47 is four tens, so six times it is twenty-four tens.

## Common stumbles

- Writes both products down whole, so forty-seven times six becomes two thousand four hundred forty-two.
- Never adds the four carried tens, and gets two hundred forty-two.
- Treats the 4 as four ones, and gets sixty-six.

## Two concrete models

- Place-value blocks: six groups of four ten rods and seven ones, with the ones traded into tens.
- An area split into a forty part and a seven part.

## Worked example

Forty-seven times six. Six times seven is forty-two: write the two, carry four tens. Six times four tens is twenty-four tens. Add the four carried tens: twenty-eight tens. Two hundred eighty-two.

## Language to use

- multiply the ones
- carry the tens
- four tens, not four

## Language to avoid

- the standard algorithm
- partial products
- carry the surplus
