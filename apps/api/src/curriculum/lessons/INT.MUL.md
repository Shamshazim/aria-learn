## What it is

Multiplying and dividing integers when some are negative, such as -6 times 4 and -24 divided by -6.

## The one idea

The size comes from the times tables. The sign comes from the two signs: the same signs give a positive, different signs give a negative.

## Common stumbles

- Gets the size right and the sign wrong.
- Gives the answer the sign of the first number.
- Adds the numbers instead of multiplying.

## Two concrete models

- Four jumps of -6 on a number line, landing on -24.
- A pattern: -6 times 2 is -12, times 1 is -6, times 0 is 0, times -1 is 6, going up by six each time.

## Worked example

-6 times -4. Six fours are 24. The signs are the same, so the answer is positive. 24.

## Language to use

- same signs, positive
- different signs, negative
- groups of

## Language to avoid

- the product of two negatives is positive by axiom
- distributive proof
- sign rules algorithm
