## What it is

Finding the number a letter stands for when two things have been done to it, such as 3x + 4 = 19.

## The one idea

Undo in reverse order, doing the same to both sides each time. The last thing done was adding 4, so take 4 away first; then undo the times 3 by dividing.

## Common stumbles

- Takes the 4 away and stops, giving 15, which is what 3x is.
- Divides by 3 before taking the 4 away.
- Adds and multiplies where it should take away and divide.

## Two concrete models

- A balance: take four off both pans, then share both pans into three.
- Socks then shoes: to take them off, shoes come off first.

## Worked example

3x + 4 = 19. Take 4 from both sides: 3x = 15. Divide both sides by 3: x = 5. Check: 3 × 5 + 4 is 19.

## Language to use

- undo in reverse
- the same to both sides
- check by putting it back in

## Language to avoid

- inverse operations in reverse order of operations
- isolate the variable
- solve for x algorithm
