## What it is

Finding the mean and the median of a short list of numbers.

## The one idea

The mean shares the total out evenly: add them up and divide by how many there are. The median is the middle one once they are put in order.

## Common stumbles

- Takes the middle number of the list before sorting it.
- Gives the median when asked for the mean, or the other way round.
- Adds the numbers up and forgets to share them out.

## Two concrete models

- Towers of cubes evened out to the same height, for the mean.
- The numbers written on cards and laid in order, for the median.

## Worked example

Mean of 2, 3 and 10: the total is 15, shared by 3 numbers is 5. Median of 2, 9, 1, 7, 5: in order 1, 2, 5, 7, 9, and the middle one is 5.

## Language to use

- share it out evenly
- put them in order
- the middle one

## Language to avoid

- arithmetic mean
- measure of central tendency
- statistical distribution
