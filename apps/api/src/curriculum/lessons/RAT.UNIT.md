## What it is

Finding how much for one: 48 km in 4 hours is how many km in 1 hour.

## The one idea

A unit rate is the amount for one. Share the amount equally among the units: 48 shared into 4 is 12.

## Common stumbles

- Multiplies the numbers and gets 192.
- Takes one from the other and gets 44.
- Goes down one hour and takes one off the amount, getting 45.

## Two concrete models

- A double number line from 0 to 4 hours above 0 to 48 km, cut into four equal jumps.
- A bar of 48 split into four equal parts.

## Worked example

48 km in 4 hours. Share 48 into 4 equal hours. Each hour is 12 km.

## Language to use

- for one
- each
- share equally

## Language to avoid

- unit rate formula
- the constant of proportionality
- rate quotient
